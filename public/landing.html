Is InvoiceDotCom fully GST compliant?

Yes! Fully compliant with Indian GST regulations. We support CGST, SGST, IGST, and cess calculations with proper HSN/SAC code support meeting all government formatting rules.

How does the Khatabook Party Ledger work?

You can record GAVE (credit) and GOT (debit) entries for each party. Track outstanding balances, view passbook-style ledgers, and send WhatsApp reminders for pending payments.

Can I print invoices on thermal printers?

Absolutely! InvoiceDotCom supports both classic A4/A5 paper formats and standard POS thermal 2-inch and 3-inch printers for retail checkout counters.
